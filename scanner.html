LOCAL TERMINAL COMMAND

Know what a match means

Daily setups use closing prices. These rules do not use daily intraday highs and lows.

DAILY SETUPMATCH CONDITION
20-day breakoutThe last close is above every close in the preceding 20 sessions.
Bull flagMore than a 10% rise over the 10 sessions before the last five, followed by five closes within a 5% range.
Reclaim MA20The previous close was below its 20-day average; the last close is above it.
OversoldWilder RSI 14 is below 30.
Oversold bounceRSI 14 was below 30 yesterday and the latest session closed up.

The intraday rule

The default range uses the 9:30, 9:35 and 9:40 AM New York bars. The first completed five-minute bar to close above the highest high of those bars matches the opening-range breakout rule. The earliest confirmation is 9:50 AM.

Stage 1 screens 5-minute closes in batches. Stage 2 confirms candidates using actual highs and lows. With a confirmation cap, the most traded candidates are checked first and skipped candidates are reported as partial coverage.

A repeatable daily workflow

1

Refresh the universe

Run the universe command about once a week. It writes trader/universe.txt, one SYMBOL|Name per line.

2

Prepare before the open

Run the daily scan. Review the number fetched, scanned and matched, plus failures. Results are ordered by price × volume.

3

Confirm during the session

Run the opening-range scan after 9:50 AM New York time. Use a focused symbol list or watchlist for a smaller scan.

4

Review before proceeding

Choose a chart to investigate. Continue with News Monitor and Entry Timer. Re-run the scan when prices have moved; a previous match can become stale.

COVERAGE

Partial means partial

Check failed, missing and skipped chart counts. A capped or incomplete scan is not the whole market.

TIMING

Session-aware rules

Incomplete and off-grid bars are excluded. The scanner applies session times and its bundled 2026–2027 holiday calendar.

The opening-range scan writes nothing before its first confirmation window, on non-trading days, after the close or after the configured entry cutoff. Missing five-minute bars can make a chart unusable. Market calendars outside the bundled years need review.

This tool does not place, size or approve orders. A historical setup or rule match cannot guarantee an outcome.
